**Hexeosis** has been producing geometric digital art since 2013, building a body of work defined by mathematical precision, infinite loopability, and a cool, abstract visual language that feels equally at home in gallery installations and VJ sets.

Their *HX-01* project — referenced directly on the official site and distributed via their YouTube channel — is representative of the Hexeosis aesthetic: geometric forms cycling through elegant transformations, each frame precise, each loop seamless. The work draws on a tradition of screen-saver culture and demoscene aesthetics while operating squarely within the contemporary VJ loop ecosystem.

The Hexeosis catalogue is built for use: clips are designed for layering, blending modes, and loop-synced playback. Their Instagram reel output demonstrates a consistent visual identity across spiral geometries, grid-based animations, and abstract light systems.

For VJs working with geometric or abstract aesthetics, Hexeosis is a reference point worth knowing.
