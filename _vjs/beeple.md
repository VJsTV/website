**Beeple** (Mike Winkelmann) is one of the most recognised names in digital art — a South Carolina-based artist whose *Everydays* project, 5,000 daily rendered images created without a single missed day from 2007 to 2021, became a landmark in both creative discipline and digital culture. The culminating collage sold at Christie's for $69 million, making it one of the highest-priced works by a living artist ever auctioned.

Beyond the headline, Beeple's live visuals practice is substantial. He has created stage visuals for **Justin Bieber**, **Nicki Minaj**, **deadmau5**, **Imagine Dragons**, and **One Direction**, and his real-time concert graphics — often dystopian, satirical, hyperdense CGI — established a visual language that has been widely imitated across the live events industry.

His tools are Cinema 4D, Octane Render, After Effects, and ZBrush, combined with a relentless output ethic. He distributes much of his work freely on Vimeo, making his catalogue one of the most accessible reference libraries in the field.

Beeple's practice exists at the intersection of commercial live visuals, fine art NFTs, and daily creative discipline — an unusual position that makes him relevant across the VJ, motion graphics, and digital art communities simultaneously.
