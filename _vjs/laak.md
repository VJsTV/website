**LAAK** is the working name of Bart Verlaak, a Belgian motion designer and VJ whose output combines polished geometric loops with an unusually candid public presence about the profession. His Vimeo channel collects both self-directed visual work and client projects, demonstrating range across abstract loop content and more narrative-driven live visual design.

Verlaak has been open about the practical side of VJing — rates, workflow, client relationships — in interviews and online conversations, making him a respected voice in the community beyond his visual output alone. The YouTube interview linked from his profile offers a useful window into how a working European VJ navigates the commercial and creative sides of the field.

His Instagram (@laakvisuals) documents ongoing work, new loops, and live contexts, while the Vimeo channel serves as the primary archive of finished pieces. LAAK represents the thoughtful, craft-oriented end of motion design applied to live visual performance.
