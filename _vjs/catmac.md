**Catmac** is the alias of Marcos Fernández Díaz, a Spanish digital artist and stage visuals designer whose work spans 3D graphics, abstract VJ loops, and free content distribution. He has published more than **450 free loops on Vimeo**, making him one of the more prolific free-content contributors in the VJ community.

His official site (catmac.art) describes the practice in plain terms: stage visuals, digital art, and free VJ loops. The work leans toward clean 3D abstraction and geometric forms rendered with high production quality, suited to electronic music events, festivals, and installation contexts.

Catmac's Instagram (@catmac_visuals) and Facebook (catmacvisuals) pages are regularly updated with new work and release announcements. The combination of a substantial free library and consistent output makes him a well-regarded resource in the VJ and stage design community, particularly among performers looking for high-quality 3D loop content without licence barriers.
