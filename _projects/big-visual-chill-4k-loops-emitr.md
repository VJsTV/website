**Big Visual Chill** produces 4K VJ loops and live wallpapers with an emphasis on atmospheric, ambient visual content. The catalogue spans geometric abstractions, organic forms, and motion-driven backgrounds suitable for live VJ performance, installation, and ambient display contexts.

Alongside the content library, BVC has developed **EMITR**, a tool designed for visual performers that extends standard loop playback functionality. The combination of high-quality loop production and tool development makes BVC a notable independent creative studio in the VJ community.

Browse the full catalogue and EMITR at [the-bvc.com](https://the-bvc.com) or the [YouTube channel](https://www.youtube.com/bigvisualchill).
