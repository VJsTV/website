**VJ Survival Kit** is a long-form interview with **LAAK** (Bart Verlaak), a Belgian motion designer and VJ. The conversation covers the practical realities of professional VJ work — how to price gigs, manage client relationships, build a sustainable workflow, and navigate the gap between creative ambition and commercial reality.

Verlaak's willingness to discuss the business side of VJing openly makes this one of the more useful public documents in the field. Watch it on [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=61Aq83w7uzU). His full loop catalogue is on [Vimeo](https://vimeo.com/laakvisuals).
