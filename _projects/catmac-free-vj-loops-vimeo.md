**Catmac** (Marcos Fernández Díaz) has built one of the more substantial free loop libraries in the VJ community, with over **450 free VJ loops** published on Vimeo. The work focuses on 3D graphics and geometric abstractions rendered with professional quality — content designed for stage visuals, electronic music events, and festival contexts.

The library is freely available without licence barriers, making Catmac's Vimeo channel a practical resource for performing VJs who need high-quality 3D loop content. New work is announced regularly on Instagram (@catmac_visuals) and Facebook (catmacvisuals).

Full catalogue at [catmac.art](https://www.catmac.art) and [Vimeo](https://vimeo.com/catmacvisuals).
