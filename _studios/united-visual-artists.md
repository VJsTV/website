**United Visual Artists (UVA)** was founded in London in 2003 and has since become one of the most significant art and technology practices operating at the intersection of live performance and permanent installation. Their work is defined by the relationship between light, time, perception, and presence — an unusually philosophical starting point for a studio whose output also includes spectacular concert stage designs and public commissions.

Their *Momentum* series — kinetic light sculptures that manipulate the perception of time and motion — and *Synchronicity*, an installation exploring the emergent behaviour of synchronised oscillators, demonstrate the range of UVA's practice: from the intimate to the architectural, from gallery to arena.

UVA's live performance portfolio includes visuals and stage design for **Massive Attack**, **Chemical Brothers**, and **U2**. Their technical infrastructure — custom C++ and OpenGL software, bespoke LED systems, spatial audio integration — is entirely in-house, giving them creative control over every aspect of the work.

The studio maintains a permanent presence at major international venues and festivals, and has received widespread institutional recognition including commissions from **Tate**, **the Barbican**, and cultural institutions across Europe and North America.
