**Studio Rewind** has been producing motion design and live show visuals from Rotterdam since 2011. Their work occupies the professional production tier of the live visuals industry — full concept-to-delivery packages for touring musicians and global brands, executed with a high level of 3D animation and VFX craft.

Their live show portfolio demonstrates a clear specialisation in artist-specific visual identities: bespoke 3D animated content built around a musician's aesthetic and performance structure, designed to run cleanly at arena scale across a full touring cycle. The *Feed Me* tour visuals are a representative example — custom 3D animation built to complement the artist's electronic music aesthetic, rendered for large-format stage display.

The studio's approach is concept-first: before a frame is rendered, the visual language is agreed with the client as part of a coherent creative direction. This distinguishes Studio Rewind from generalist VFX houses and positions them firmly within the live visuals and concert touring ecosystem.

Clients span electronic music, festival culture, and commercial brand work across Europe and internationally.
