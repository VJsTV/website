**Frantic** is a London-based motion design and animation studio with a deep portfolio spanning broadcast advertising, music industry content, cultural institutions, and digital brand campaigns. Their CG and motion graphics work operates at production-house quality — the kind of output that ends up in major broadcast slots and international ad campaigns.

The studio's output — available through their YouTube channel and Behance portfolio — demonstrates mastery across character animation, abstract CG systems, product visualisation, and typographic motion work. Their stylistic range is wide, but a consistent commitment to craft quality runs through everything they produce.

Within the VJ and live visuals ecosystem, Frantic represents the high-end motion design adjacent to performance — studios whose aesthetic vocabulary and technical pipeline inform the visual language of stages, screens, and LED installations even when the studio itself is not a VJ practice. Their work is a reference point for quality in the broader audiovisual production world.

Based in London with an international client base, Frantic is part of the established UK motion design scene alongside studios such as **Mainframe**, **Nexus**, and **ManvsMachine**.
